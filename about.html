About KITE

KITE is not just another WordPress theme—it's a next-generation block-based toolkit for creators, designers, and developers who want full control, creative freedom, and optimal performance directly in the WordPress full site editor.

Our Mission

We believe that building beautiful, fast, and customizable websites should be simple and accessible to everyone. With KITE, you can design every part of your website visually, without relying on clunky page builders or endless plugins. Our goal is to empower you to create unique experiences, faster than ever.

What Makes KITE Unique?

  • 100% Block-Based: True full site editing—change headers, footers, templates & more visually.
  • Performance-First: Lightweight, fast-loading, and optimized for speed out of the box.
  • Pixel-Perfect Design: Modern layouts, beautiful patterns, and a curated block library.
  • Endless Customization: Tweak every detail directly in the editor—no code required (unless you want to!).
  • Seamless Integration: Works with your favorite WordPress plugins and tools.

Who Is KITE For?

KITE is perfect for freelancers, agencies, businesses, bloggers, and anyone who wants to build a unique WordPress site without compromise. Whether you're a pro or just getting started, KITE puts powerful design tools at your fingertips.
